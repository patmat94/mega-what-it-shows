The three problems

parser: day and month swapped

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April goes out as 4 July. Reports come in by app, email and form from every tenant. Fixing the parser fixes these, and only these.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order

On tenants running booking-v3, the form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way, day first. The date comes out swapped just like parser, which is why it was merged. But a parser fix does not touch it: the form hint has to match the tenant's locale. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants land here.

one-day: the invite lands one day off

These are not swaps. “Tuesday 12 May” arrives as “Monday 11 May”; the same meeting shows Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before its booking was made. That is a time zone shift, not a date format. The desk noticed them (desk-notes.json) but CauseTable files them under parser by tenant, so they would stay broken after the parser fix.

How they got merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone, and triage says “when unsure, merge”. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Month and day swapped?”), so they merged into one ticket. Intake's tag is no better: it guesses from the reporter's browser, not the tenant, and disagrees with the cause on many reports (see “tagged as” above).

Merging on subject needs a new decision record that replaces 0001. Until then: before merging into a big ticket, check the cause, not the subject.
